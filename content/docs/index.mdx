title: Welcome
description: Lumen is an iPhone browser with a private library of what you read, and a small model on the phone to answer questions about it.
---

Lumen is a web browser for iPhone. As you read, it saves the pages you spend time on into a library on your phone, sorted by topic and by website, and a small language model running on the device answers questions from what you saved. Nothing you save or ask goes to a server, and there is no account to create.

These pages cover getting the app, what happens the first time you open it, how the library and Ask work, what the app sends over the network, and how the code fits together. Start with whichever one matches your question, or read them in the order of the sidebar.
